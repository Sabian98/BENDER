== Settings
T 300 K, ionic strength 150 mM, kT 2.4943 kJ/mol. B2 = -2 pi int (exp(-u/kT) - 1) r^2 dr, nm^3; dB2 = B2(Mpipi-Recharged) - B2(CALVADOS-2), so dB2 < 0 means more attractive under Mpipi-Recharged.
CALVADOS-2 (calvados 0.9.0): Ashbaugh-Hatch eps = 0.2 kcal/mol = 0.8368 kJ/mol, lambda and sigma arithmetic means, cutoff 2 nm (shifted); Debye-Hueckel eps_yu 1.7875 kJ/mol nm, kappa 1.2755 /nm, cutoff 4 nm (shifted); charges R, K +1, D, E -1, H 0.0307 (pH 7.5). Terminal charges are not residue-pair terms and are left out (CALVADOS-2 charges both termini, Mpipi-Recharged neither).
Mpipi-Recharged (OpenMpipi 0.1.0): Wang-Frenkel (pair eps, sigma, mu; nu = 1; zero beyond 3 sigma) plus Yukawa A_ij exp(-kappa r)/r, kappa 1.2755 /nm, truncated without shift at 3.5 nm. The A table gives His an effective charge of about +0.51 (A_HH / A_KK = 0.259); Arg-acid A is 1.14 times Lys-acid A.
Check against OpenMM (Reference platform, two beads): 6 pairs x 11 distances (0.45-3.6 nm) per force field, max |difference| 1.8e-08 kJ/mol (b2_openmm_check.csv). B2 integration: halving dr and starting at 0.03 nm changes B2 by at most 4.6e-12 nm^3.
CALVADOS IDP/prepare.py sets neither eps_lj nor the cutoffs, so the package defaults above apply (assumes the calvados on the cluster has the defaults of the local install).
Sequences: BENDER_BIO six groups; 4 X residues taken as Gly (as BENDER_BIO and both simulations).

== Reading
A single physical feature, the pair-weighted mean dB2, with ln N predicts 0.362 of var(dnu), as much as the 29-term composition model (0.362); with the CALVADOS-2 mean B2 and squares and products (9 terms) it reaches 0.483, the 57-term composition model being 0.483. The two overlap but are not the same: together they give 0.531 (shared 0.436, about 90% of either). Splitting dB2 into 11 chemical classes gives 0.413, against 0.345 for the same class pair fractions without B2 weights, so the B2 weights, not the grouping of pairs, carry the signal. Weighting pairs by ideal-chain contact probability lowers every B2 model (dB2 + ln N 0.311): the disagreement follows interactions between all residue pairs, not sequence-local ones. A gradient-boosting check gives the same picture (3 B2 features 0.534, 29 composition features 0.562). Mean pair B2 explains less of nu itself within either force field than composition does (see the within-force-field line above), so B2 describes the disagreement better than it describes nu. In the contested class the 9-term B2 model scores 0.570 out of fold (composition 0.531) and predicts a class mean of -0.028 (observed -0.037).

== Caveats
Sampling noise (block SE of both nu) is 10.1% of var(dnu), so the ceiling for any model is about 0.90; the best models here reach 0.53, leaving about 0.37 systematic but unexplained (sequence patterning, terminal charges, many-body effects are candidates).
Part of the B2-composition overlap is built in: the mean dB2 is the pair fractions weighted by fixed dB2 values. The informative result is that fixed weights from the potentials, with 2-9 fitted coefficients, match fits of 29-57 coefficients, and beat the unweighted class fractions.
This is still a regression on dnu; the parameter-swap simulation is the causal test. B2 is a dilute two-body measure of each pair potential and ignores chain connectivity and many-body screening.
CALVADOS-2 terminal charges (+1 N, -1 C) are not in the residue-pair table; the termini control runs will show their share of dnu.

== Draft mechanism paragraph (JCTC Results)
To ask whether these differences are already written into the potentials, we computed the second virial coefficient $B_2$ of the non-bonded pair potential of each force field for all 210 residue pairs at 300 K and 150 mM. Arginine--aromatic and aromatic--aromatic pairs are close to ideal under CALVADOS-2 ($|B_2| \le 0.10$ nm$^3$) but strongly attractive under Mpipi-Recharged ($B_2$ from $-0.8$ to $-2.4$ nm$^3$), whereas lysine--aromatic and aliphatic pairs are more repulsive under Mpipi-Recharged, and histidine, nearly neutral in CALVADOS-2 at pH 7.5 but partially charged in Mpipi-Recharged, becomes attractive to acidic and repulsive to basic residues. Averaged over the residue pairs of each sequence, the difference $\Delta B_2$ predicts 36\% of the variance of $\Delta\nu$ under the same cross-validation, as much as the linear compositional model, and together with the CALVADOS-2 $B_2$ and their squares and products it predicts 48\% with 9 terms, as much as the 57-term compositional model; combined, the two reach 53\%, so that about 90\% of what either explains is shared. In the contested class the mean $\Delta B_2$ is lower than in the other sequences by 0.028 nm$^3$, all of it from the arginine--aromatic term, and the $B_2$ model predicts $\Delta\nu$ there slightly better than composition (out-of-fold $R^2 = 0.57$ against 0.53); the compositional dependence of the disagreement is therefore largely written into the pair potentials, above all into the arginine--aromatic and aromatic--aromatic attractions that Mpipi-Recharged strengthens.
